YIWUSUPPLY

Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

YiwuSupply is an independent, local-first sourcing companion. The current mobile release does not require an online account and does not send personal or sourcing data to YiwuSupply or TC Safa servers.

Data stored on the device

The app stores shops, products, offers, collections, visits, notes, photos, and app settings in app-private storage on the phone. Optional AI-provider API keys are held using platform secure storage. Removing the app removes its local app data according to the phone platform's behavior.

Device permissions

  • Camera and photo access are used only when the user captures or selects sourcing images.
  • Foreground location is optional and is used during Quick Capture to save a shop coordinate. The app does not request background location.
  • Internet access is used only for a user-requested AI analysis.

AI provider keys

The Settings screen can store optional keys for OpenAI, Claude/Anthropic, Google Gemini, and DeepSeek. Nothing is sent automatically. When the user selects a provider and taps Analyze, YiwuSupply sends the current typed capture context directly to that provider. OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini may also receive compressed copies of up to four selected capture photos; DeepSeek receives typed context only. The provider processes that content under the user's account and its own terms and privacy policy. Suggestions remain editable and are stored locally only when the user saves.

The app does not keep a separate server copy of an AI request or response. The selected provider may retain account and request data according to its own terms. Users can revoke future AI use by clearing the provider key in Settings and not using Analyze; provider-side access, retention, or deletion requests must be managed through the user's account with that provider.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current release contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. YiwuSupply does not sell user data.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions may be sent to dev@tc-safa.com. This policy will be updated before any future cloud account, sync, server storage, or analytics becomes active.

YiwuSupply is an independent buyer tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yiwu International Trade Market.
