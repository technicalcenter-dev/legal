Portal Keyboard Data Credits

This list applies to Portal Keyboard for iPhone and iPad starting with build 111. The referenced datasets retain their own licenses.

Chinese Pinyin

Pinyin frequency and pronunciation data are from the Android Open Source Project, copyright © 2009 The Android Open Source Project, under the Apache License 2.0. Portal converts rows marked for ordinary use from UTF-16 to UTF-8 TSV, omits GBK extension rows and scales the frequency scores to integers to preserve their order. The bundle includes the upstream license and notices. Original Pinyin dataset.

English and Arabic

The English spelling list is the LibreOffice US English Hunspell dictionary, based on SCOWL, under the notices included in the bundle. Dictionary and license notices.

English and Arabic word frequency tables are by Hermit Dave and contributors to FrequencyWords, based on OpenSubtitles 2018. The files are unchanged apart from their names and are available separately under CC BY-SA 4.0. Download the exact English and Arabic plaintext tables from their source.

Emoji

The bundled catalogue uses Unicode emoji sequences and CLDR annotations under the Unicode License v3. Portal normalizes the data for local search and adds search aliases. The system renders the glyphs on your device.
